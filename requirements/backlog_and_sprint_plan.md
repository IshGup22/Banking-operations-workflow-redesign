# Agile backlog & sprint delivery plan — illustrative

## Product goal
Reduce avoidable manual reconciliation reviews while retaining traceability for exceptions.

## Prioritization
- **Sprint 1 (foundation):** US-01, US-02, US-03, US-05; clarify sample fields and core acceptance criteria
- **Sprint 2 (controls):** US-04, US-06, US-07, US-10; build rule precedence and UAT coverage
- **Sprint 3 (delivery):** US-08, US-09, US-11, US-12; summary, traceability and business readiness

## Demonstration ceremonies (not actual team attendance)
- Sprint planning: prioritize acceptance criteria and dependencies
- Daily stand-up template: yesterday / today / blockers
- Backlog refinement: revisit tolerance, age trigger and duplicate handling
- Sprint review: demonstrate classified exports and test evidence
- Retrospective: note missing acceptance criteria and estimate limitations

## Definition of done
Source and target mapping documented; rule test passes; export includes reason; RTM link verified; scope and limitations disclosed.
